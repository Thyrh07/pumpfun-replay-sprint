Fresh-launch breakout bot for make.fun's Pump.fun Replay Sprint: buys pump.fun
curves within minutes of launch while early buy pressure accelerates, sizes
small (0.1-0.2 SOL), and exits in stages — half at +60%, the rest at +150% or
on a trailing stop, hard stop at -30%, time stop. No pyramid, no chasing
coins that already ran. Paper money only; the replay scores net PnL over 24h
of recorded pump.fun activity.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
